About Me

Photo of me:

Hi! I'm Kyo (they/them) and I am the owner and one-person-army of this shop! I am currently 20 years old and working full time as an IT specialist.

I created Kyo's Delivery Service as a cozy place where I can share my passion.

At my shop, you'll find digital arts portraying different characters from different fandoms.

However, I've only been a "fanart" artist since end of 2024. I used to only draw my OCs, so the shift to fanarts was a huge jump — now I need to make characters recognizable in my drawings :3

Art has always been my way of expressing myself and connecting with people. When I was younger (as if I still aren't xD), being into Anime was considered weird and got a lot of comments. But I stayed loyal to the style and keep drawing in an anime-inspired way.

Thanks for stopping by! Have a look around and if you have any questions, feel free to reach out! I'd love to help out!

WHEN DID I START ALL OF THIS?

The Beginning, end of 2024

I've started planning this shop for over a year. But with a huge upgrade in my drawing equipment and encouragement from my besties (thx for that :3), I decided to take matters into my own hands and actually do all the steps necessary to put up a shop.
