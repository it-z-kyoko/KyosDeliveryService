About Me

20

IT
Specialist

Hi, I’m Kyo (they/them), a creative person with a passion for almost all things creative. I love to explore different mediums and express myself through art, photography and cosplay.

My cosplays mainly focus on Games and Anime. I enjoy bringing characters I love to life and meeting people with the same interests at conventions. I also enjoy taking photos of other cosplayers and capturing the essence of their characters, the sole reason i started photography - to allow others to have their hard work captured in a safe space.

What this portfolio is about

A home and overview for the things I create

This site is a home for the work I create and the projects I care about. Some of them are visual, some technical, and some live exactly between those worlds. It is a place to share finished pieces, experiments, ideas, and the process behind them.

I love building things that feel expressive and personal — whether that means capturing a moment through photography, creating a cosplay or drawing digital art.

Over time, this portfolio will grow into a broader collection of selected work, snapshots, and projects that show what I enjoy making most.
