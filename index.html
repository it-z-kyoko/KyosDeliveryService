About Me

Photo of me:
Fake Image

Hi! I'm Kyo (they/them) and I am the owner and one-person-army of this shop! I am currently 20 years old and working full time as an it specialist
I created Kyo's Delivery Service as a cozy place where I can share my passion
At my shop, you'll find digital arts portraying different characters from different fandoms.
However, I've only been a "fanart" artist since end of 2024. I've ever only drawn my OCs before, so the shift to fanarts was a huge jump, since I have a character that should be recognisable in my drawings :3
Art has always been my way of expressing myself and connecting with people. Back when I was young (as if I still aren't xD), being interested in Anime was considered weird and often times the cause of unwanted commentary. But no matter what, I've stayed loyal to the style and continue drawing in an anime-inspired style.
Thanks for stopping by! Have a look around and if you have any questions, feel free to reach out! I'd love to help out
